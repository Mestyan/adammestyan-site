Introduction

A historian between empires

Adam Mestyan is Ford Foundation Professor of Middle Eastern Studies at Harvard University. His research focuses on modern Syria and Egypt, especially the political and cultural history of the late Ottoman and interwar periods.

He spends several months every year in the region itself — in Egypt, Lebanon, Turkey, and, more recently, Syria — working across Arabic, Ottoman Turkish, French, German, and Hungarian archival sources.
