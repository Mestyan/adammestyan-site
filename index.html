Adam Mestyan
Historian of the Modern Middle East · Harvard University

I study the history of the modern Middle East, particularly the Arab provinces of the late Ottoman Empire and its successor states. My current research examines how globalization and war interacted to shape state formation and economic life in the post-Ottoman regions, especially in Egypt and Syria. I am on sabbatical leave in AY 2026–2027 as a Guggenheim Fellow and a Visiting Professor at Sciences Po, Paris.

About

A historian between empires

Hello, I'm Adam Mestyan — a historian of the modern Middle East and Ford Foundation Professor of Middle Eastern Studies at Harvard University.

My research focuses on modern Syria and Egypt, especially the political and cultural history of the late Ottoman and interwar periods. I spend several months every year in the region itself — in Egypt, Lebanon, Turkey, and, more recently, Syria.
